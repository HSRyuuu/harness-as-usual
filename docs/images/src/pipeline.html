run-work — one pipeline, a condition per row

A declaration, not a procedure. Every step is owned by its own skill; the matrix only says which steps apply, at what strength, and which gates stand between them. Every path stops at the request boundary recorded at the door.

gathering-context → investigate? → write-requirements? → write-plan → execute-plan → review-execution? → cleanup-code? → finalize → git-action?
Step skill
Applies when
Strength
1
gathering-contextalways
Always — the first step of every record.
Settle what later steps need: scope, constraints, acceptance for a change; symptoms, impact, reproduction conditions for an investigation. Zero questions is normal.
2
investigatewhen needed
A cause, direction, or feasibility must be established from code, logs, or an experiment before anything changes — including a bug whose cause is unconfirmed, however small the fix.
Read-only by default. Ends in one of its three endings: conclusion only · carry on · split.
◼ Investigation only
stops here: the answer is written to conclusion.md and goes straight to finalize.
3
write-requirementswhen needed
The requirements need agreeing: a choice between viable approaches; a contract or product surface — public API, data model, auth, migration, deployment, dependency policy, user-facing wording, business rules; hard to reverse or verify; built around a high-risk operation. Size is not the test.
A full requirements.md. Never for a bug whose cause is unconfirmed — the cause is found, not agreed.
4
write-planthe work changes code
The work changes code.
A full plan.md when requirements.md exists; checklist strength — steps and verification method — otherwise. Ends in the pre-approval critical review either way.
◼ Plan only
stops here: the reviewed plan waits at awaiting-user. There is nothing to finalize yet.
🔒 Gate
A successful write-plan review newer than the previous approval, then the user's explicit execution approval. High-risk operations need a fresh approval of their own.
5
execute-planuser approved
The user approved execution of the reviewed plan.
Evidence in verification.md when requirements.md exists; otherwise only when the evidence needs more than the record's summaries.
6
review-executionproposed or offered
Execution finished.
Proposed by default when requirements.md exists; otherwise offered when the change is broad or touched something delicate. You decide.
7
cleanup-codeapproval only
You explicitly approve cleanup.
Behavior-preserving, then re-verified.
🔒 Gate
No completion claim without verification evidence that matches the surface — and INCONCLUSIVE is not PASS.
8
finalizerequired or offered
Required with conclusion.md, or an approved execution with requirements.md. Offered for an approved execution without requirements.md — a passing verification already completes it. Neither: the helper refuses.
report.md, then the record is sealed. No report.md when the conclusion is the whole deliverable.
9
git-actionyour choice only
You explicitly choose one: none · commit · commit + push · commit + push + PR.
Not asked by default when the work ended in a conclusion only — there is usually nothing to commit.
◼ Execute
runs through to here — and never on into release or deploy.
Re-read as it learns
A change that looked settled and turns out to need a decision, touch a contract surface, or be hard to reverse enters write-requirements before the plan is approved.
applies conditional — its row says when a request boundary stops a gate the helper refuses to pass
Gathering finds nothing to do? That finding is the record's whole output: it goes in the Decisions band and the record closes as cancelled with its reason.  ·  An investigation that finds nothing wrong is different — it ends with a conclusion.md.
