Skill layer

13 runtime skills, five jobs

One entry point opens the record. One owner declares the pipeline. One skill talks to you. Eight steps do the work and never branch on what kind of work called them. Two utilities are available to anyone.

Entry
the single door
using-as-usual
Decides whether the harness applies at all, creates or resumes the work folder, records the request boundary, and hands off. Owns no pipeline of its own.
Owner
a declaration, not a procedure — one matrix of which steps apply, at what strength, behind which gates
run-work
The owner of every work record. Derives state with status --json and routes each phase to its step skill by what the work needs.
Gathering
the first step of every record, and the only skill that interviews you
gathering-context
Settles the open items, recommends an answer with every question, and writes what was agreed into contexts.md for you.
Steps
shared and condition-agnostic — strength comes from the matrix and the caller, never from a branch inside the step
investigate read-only
Runs the investigation loop and ends it: a conclusion, a carry-on into the change, or a split.
write-requirements
Turns the agreed context into one reviewable requirements.md — you are its reviewer.
write-plan
Writes the execution contract, then reviews it critically and fixes what it finds before asking you to approve.
execute-plan
Executes the approved plan in order and records each task's verification evidence. Delegation is its call; the evidence is not.
review-execution
Reviews the real changes against what was asked. Findings land in review.md and reach a disposition.
cleanup-code approval only
Behavior-preserving improvement of the change surface, then re-verified.
finalize
Checks the record can carry a fresh session, optionally proposes a skill improvement, writes report.md, then seals it.
git-action your choice only
Runs the action you picked — none, commit, commit + push, or commit + push + PR.
Utilities
not workflow phases — they add no phase and no next action
explore-codebase read-only
Finds repository facts — affected files, code flow, interfaces, tests, local conventions — by reading.
manage-self-improvement
Proposes project-local skill updates for reusable procedures learned from the work, then applies the approved ones.
The rule that keeps this honest: differences in strength live in the owner's matrix and in what the caller passes — never inside a step. A step that branches on what kind of work called it is the exact defect this design removes.
