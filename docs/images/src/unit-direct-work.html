Work unit — 2 of 3

direct-work — what to do is already settled

Clear, low-risk, reversible development. Agreeing requirements would be ceremony, but the work is still worth a record. Often asks you nothing at all.

Step 1 · zero questions is normal
gathering-context
If nothing is open, that fact is recorded and the work moves on.
→
Step 2 · checklist strength
write-plan
Steps plus how each one gets verified. Not a full plan document.
🔒 Gate
Lighter than a topic's, but it happens: the checklist is read critically once and fixed before you approve execution.
Step 3
execute-plan
The verification must exercise the changed behavior — "it compiles" is not evidence.
→
Step 4 · optional
review-execution
Offered when the change was broad or touched something delicate.
→
Step 5 · optional
cleanup-code
Behavior-preserving cleanup, re-verified. Explicit approval only.
🔒 Gate
No completion without verification evidence that matches the surface — and INCONCLUSIVE is not PASS.
Step 6 · optional
finalize
Offered at the end. The record is already complete without it.
→
Step 7 · your choice only
git-action
none · commit · commit + push · commit + push + PR. Never one you did not pick.
Ends with
a code change whose last recorded event is a passing verification
Route back for reclassification when gathering reveals a decision between viable approaches, a contract or product surface (API, data model, auth, migration, deployment, business rules), work that is hard to reverse or verify, or an unconfirmed cause behind it — that last one is an issue.
required optional — offered, never assumed a gate the record helper refuses to pass, rather than warns about
Artifacts: contexts.md · audit.jsonl · plan.md — plus review.md/report.md only if those steps run.  ·  Size is not the test: a mechanical rename across thirty files belongs here; a two-line change to how sessions expire does not.
