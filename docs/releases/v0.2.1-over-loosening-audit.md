# v0.2.1 — The Over-Loosening Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Previous:** 0.2.0 · **Commits:** `ed03a28`..`fe0a394`

> Reconstructed from git history on 2026-08-25.

v2 rewrote the runtime surface for a frontier model and, in trimming prose, took
gates with it. This release is the audit that found that and the fixes that
followed. It is the reason `AGENTS.md` now states the rule directly: when
adapting AsUsual for weaker models, tighten the judgment layer; **never loosen
the record layer.**

## What Was Restored

- **The gates the refactoring dropped**, in two passes — general gates first,
  then the record-integrity rules specifically.
- **The consent door** behind the audit's one Critical finding.
- The remaining audit findings, closed as a group.

## What Was Trimmed Further

- **The runtime surface stops narrating its own freedom.** Text explaining what
  the agent is free to *not* do is a design note; design notes belong in
  `AGENTS.md`, not in the rules an agent reads at runtime. This became a standing
  check in `verify-runtime-surface`.
- Judgment-layer heuristics come out of `git-action` and
  `manage-self-improvement`.
- The skills and prompts the v2 pass had left untouched are rewritten to match.

## Documentation

- The architecture doc is synced with the restored gates.
- The durable docs' script-enforcement claim is corrected — it had promised more
  than `as-usual-record.py` actually refuses.
- The private-path check is fixed to search what actually ships.
- The v2 working documents are dropped now that the audit is closed.
